### Project Description
A satirical, intentionally useless AI judge that evaluates your daily hydration habits and issues hilarious, completely unhinged verdicts with zero medical credentials. Features an interactive water glass that visually fills up with animated waves and bubbles as you adjust your intake!

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Humans have survived for millennia by simply drinking water when they feel thirsty—completely unaware that their drinking technique hasn't been officially scored out of 10 or investigated for municipal fluid crimes.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
An over-engineered comedy AI hydration court that demands your identity, logs your water intake, calculates fabricated confidence ratings, and ruthlessly roasts you as an *"Honorary Cactus"* or *"Hydration Criminal."*
